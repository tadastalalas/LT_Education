LT_Education v1.12.0 :
- Dead book vendors are now replaced by a new vendor with the same name, at the next weekly update or when loading the game.
- The new vendor starts with fresh relations. The dead one stays in the encyclopedia.
- Book vendors no longer die of old age.
- Fixed the book icon on the map never showing for Lady Pokesalot (the One-Handed novice scholar).
- Fixed scholar and vendor icons on the map staying hidden for up to a day after your scout reached 200 Scouting.
- Fixed books disappearing when a companion who was reading left your clan or died. The book now returns to your party.
- Fixed a companion keeping a book after their reading got stuck in a broken state.
- Fixed scholar training breaking when you saved and loaded during training. Training now resumes where it stopped and pays out correctly.
- Fixed the training screen showing the wrong scholar if the scholar moved to another town during your training.
- Fixed book vendors being duplicated in towns each time a town menu was opened.
- Fixed your character silently "reading" a book you had sold or lost. You now get a message and reading stops.
- Fixed the "You don't have a book you were reading before..." message repeating every hour while reading.
- Fixed the "lteducation.debug 0" console command setting the minimum Intelligence to read to 4 instead of 3 and not restoring the rest time between trainings.
- Fixed the "Can read!" map notification title not being translated.
- Fixed Alt+F12 reopening the Book Stash many times while the keys were held.
- Fixed the Book Stash crashing when opened while you were a prisoner.
- Fixed some random picks never choosing the last option (vendor spawn towns and weapon part offers).
- Fixed relation changes with book vendors sometimes applying more than once per greeting.
- Fixed possible errors after returning to the main menu and loading another game.
- Fixed weapon part purchase messages ("Selected: ..." and "You don't have enough gold...") so they can be translated.
- Fixed the Japanese "Can read!" notification saying "Can't read!".
- Fixed the Polish, Spanish and Chinese "[Read]" book tag meaning "read (now)" instead of "already read".
- Fixed read books getting a second "[Read]" tag in non-English languages.
- Fixed book vendors sometimes stocking more than 12 books.
- Fixed a possible crash when teaching weapon parts on an incompatible game version. An error message is shown instead.
- Fixed a crash when a dead book vendor was moved to another town or brought back to life.
- Fixed a possible crash when the mod wrote its first error or debug log.
- Fixed smiths saying "Come tomorrow" after starting or loading a game until a day had passed.
- Fixed a possible crash when a book vendor outside any town was moved at the weekly update.
- Fixed scholar training starting, and using up the rest time, with nobody selected.
- Fixed a possible crash in the debug book vendor location list.
- Fixed a possible crash when opening the reading menu outside a town or castle.
- Added German, French, Italian, Korean and Traditional Chinese translations.
- Completed the Brazilian Portuguese translation and translated the missing lines in all other languages.
- Fixed many wrong or broken lines in the Chinese, Japanese, Polish, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, Ukrainian and Brazilian Portuguese translations.
	Fixed the "You can't read..." message saying the opposite in all languages.
	Fixed broken text values (names, gold, line breaks) in Turkish, Spanish, Ukrainian, Japanese and Russian.
	Fixed the word "and" in settlement lists being empty in Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Fixed the Book Stash "Close" button, the "INT" label and several error messages not being translated.
- Renamed the language files to the standard names.
